About

animation, illustration, visual storytelling

Ziyu Wang is an animator working with hybrid media, combining hand-drawn textures, stop-motion materials and animated movement to create imaginative, emotionally driven visual worlds.

Her practice explores memory, childhood feeling and intimate storytelling through animation, illustration and mixed-media image-making.

She is interested in creating tender, poetic visual experiences that connect personal emotion with playful narrative form.
